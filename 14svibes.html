About

14’s Vibes is a hip-hop duo from Garhwa, consisting of artists Kafew and Dan. Known for their dynamic synergy and complementary styles, the duo blends distinct perspectives into a unified sonic identity that reflects both individuality and collaboration.

Their music explores a range of themes—from personal journeys and emotions to street realities and youthful energy—delivered through engaging flows and expressive lyricism. Kafew and Dan bring contrasting tones and creative approaches, allowing 14’s Vibes to craft tracks that feel layered, versatile, and authentic.

Driven by consistency and a shared artistic vision, 14’s Vibes continues to grow within the independent hip-hop scene, representing the emerging sound and cultural voice of their region.
