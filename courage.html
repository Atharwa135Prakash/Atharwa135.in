About

Courage (Tanishq Samant) is a hip-hop artist from Jamshedpur, recognized for his unapologetic style and raw, unfiltered lyricism. His music is rooted in authenticity, delivering hard-hitting bars that reflect real-life experiences, emotions, and perspectives without compromise.

With a strong focus on storytelling, Courage transforms personal narratives into powerful sonic expressions, blending intensity, honesty, and street-driven energy. His approach to rap is direct and fearless, resonating with listeners who value truth over trends.

Driven by passion and dedication to his craft, Courage continues to carve a distinct space in the underground hip-hop scene, representing a voice that is both real and uncompromising.
