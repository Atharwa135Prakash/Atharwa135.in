About

Kanishq Singh is an emerging hip-hop artist from Jamshedpur, known for his sharp lyrical approach and authentic storytelling rooted in real-life experiences. Blending modern hip-hop influences with a grounded, desi perspective, his music reflects themes of ambition, struggle, identity, and self-expression.

With a strong command over flow, rhythm, and wordplay, Kanishq brings a distinctive voice to the growing underground rap scene. His ability to switch between introspective narratives and high-energy performances allows him to connect with diverse audiences while staying true to his artistic core.

Driven by passion and consistency, Kanishq Singh continues to evolve as an artist, contributing to the independent hip-hop movement and representing the cultural energy of his city on a broader stage.
