About

RVX (Rajveer Singh) is a beat producer from Jamshedpur, known for crafting dynamic instrumentals that bridge modern hip-hop sound with distinct sonic textures. His production style focuses on creating immersive beats that elevate an artist’s voice while maintaining a strong standalone identity.

With a keen ear for rhythm, layering, and sound selection, RVX builds compositions that range from hard-hitting trap to melodic and atmospheric hip-hop. His approach balances technical precision with creative instinct, resulting in beats that are both versatile and impactful.

Committed to growth and consistency, RVX continues to contribute to the independent music scene, shaping sounds that resonate with both artists and listeners alike.
