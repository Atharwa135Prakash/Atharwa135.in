About

Shunyateen (Aditya Kumar Singh) is an independent hip-hop artist hailing from Daltonganj. Known for his introspective lyricism and emotionally driven narratives, his music explores themes of identity, solitude, societal realities, and personal evolution.

Blending raw expression with a minimal yet impactful sonic approach, Shunyateen crafts verses that resonate deeply with listeners seeking meaning beyond surface-level sound. His pen game reflects a balance of poetic depth and contemporary rap sensibilities, allowing him to stand out within the underground hip-hop landscape.

Rooted in authenticity and artistic integrity, Shunyateen represents a voice of reflection and resistance—continuously pushing boundaries while staying connected to his origins and truth.
